Every face here is on Google Fonts under an open licence. Headings are set in the family's heaviest available weight; body copy in its text weight, so each plate shows the family carrying both jobs on its own. Single-weight display faces (Anton, Bebas Neue, Protest Guerrilla, Workbench, Sixtyfour Convergence) have no text weight to fall back on — that is why their body copy looks loud, and why they belong in a pairing rather than alone.

Martian Mono is requested with font-stretch: condensed for headings and semi-condensed for body — Google Fonts serves it as static width instances, not a live axis.
